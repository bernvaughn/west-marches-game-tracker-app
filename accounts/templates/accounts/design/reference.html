Shared layout classes

Promoted from the area sweeps. Use these; do not add an area copy.

.wm-toolbar
A wrapping row of buttons and links.
.wm-inline-form
A form that sits in a row.
.wm-card-grid
An auto-fill grid of cards or links.
.wm-fieldset and .wm-form-row
A grouped set of fields; the row is one column on phones.
.wm-check-list
A list of checkboxes (--inline for a row).
.wm-filter-pills
A row of filter buttons or tabs.
.wm-section-bar
An h2 with an action at the right.
.wm-code-wrap
Code and tokens that wrap instead of overflow.
.wm-dl--grid
A definition list laid out as a grid of facts.
